How We Test Casinos

Our casino review process covers six stages. A casino must pass all six before it appears on this site. We re-test all listed casinos every quarter and remove any that no longer meet our standards.

01

Licence Verification

We verify every casino's licence directly with the issuing authority — UKGC, MGA, Curaçao, or other. Expired or suspended licences are an immediate disqualification.

02

Real Money Deposit

We make a real money deposit at every casino we review. We test a minimum of three payment methods and document processing times, fees, and any friction in the deposit flow.

03

Bonus Terms Audit

We read the full bonus terms, not just the headline offer. Wagering requirements, game restrictions, maximum bet rules, time limits, and maximum withdrawal caps are all documented.

04

Game Library Check

We verify the game library against provider claims. RTP figures are checked against certified game sheets from Pragmatic Play, NetEnt, BTG, and other providers directly.

05

Withdrawal Test

We request a withdrawal and document the exact time from request to funds received. We test e-wallet, card, and where available, cryptocurrency withdrawals.

06

Support Test

We contact customer support with a genuine query and document response time, accuracy, and helpfulness. Live chat, email, and where available, phone support are all tested.
